Charging station — rolls

Six seeded stations from the same generator, then one of them (click a roll) in motion with a robot entering and exiting. Four loosely triangular gem layers, each rotated against the next; layers 1–3 are rings cut into three pieces, layer 4 is solid. Six of the nine pieces are slots and light up back-to-front in the stored robot's colour as robots dock. The halo ripple runs between layers 1 and 2: inward as a robot vanishes at the centre, outward as one appears. Shading, bevel, facet tones, boundary lines and palette are the Phase 39 robot's.

Gate questions: Does it read as the same manufacturer as the robots? Do four rotated layers read as depth, or as a mess — and does per-layer bob help or hurt that? Is a 10-unit gap right (20 px at world scale)? Do lit pieces read as "robots stored", or is the port gem or a light dot the clearer count? Does entering between L2/L3 and exiting between L3/L4 read as into and out of, with the vanish timed to the inward ripple? Is one ripple skipped under overlap acceptable when 12 robots exit at world open? Accent on the front layer, or dark with only the slots and port lit?

The selected roll in a wider scene. Enter sends a free robot in from the right; Exit lets the last stored robot out to the left.

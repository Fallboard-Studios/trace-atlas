Moves and jobs — sketch

Task 0b of the Phase 43 sketch gate. One roster robot parks above a host and runs a job: bob, detach, the job's moves, reattach. Two hosts — a Refinery-style factory (hard edges, chimney mouth, mast, pipe run) and a dome (curved hull, crown, side vent, base pipe) — each with hand-placed work points and two paths. The five moves have their own buttons; the six jobs chain them per spec §1.9. Every job runs for JOB_BASE_SECONDS, 6–10 s from the tempo: slower BPM, longer job. Orbiter count only changes how many orbiters work.

Gate questions: Can each job be told apart from across the world? Does a 10 s job at 20 BPM drag, and does a 6 s job at 200 BPM read as rushed? With 1 s flights inside the duration, is ~3.3 s of work enough for a two-move job at 6 s (A1)? Does trace on the dome's curved hull read as work or as a glitch? Do the moves still read at 0.75×? Is the reduced-motion pulse enough to say "working"?

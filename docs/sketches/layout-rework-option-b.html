Layout rework — option B, "Stage & Inspector"

The alternative to option A. A covers the world and dims it. B never covers it: nav, mute, volume and telemetry live in a bottom console strip, controls live in an inspector on the right, and the selected robot is tracked in the live world instead of drawn in a separate column. Same disclaimers as A: static, approximated cabinet pop, judge structure and density. Desktop frames are 1600 × 900 at 68 %; phones are 375 × 812 at full size.

Open for Crawford (B versus A):
  1. Bottom console strip (B) or left rail + drawer (A)? The strip costs height everywhere and no width; the rail the reverse.
  2. Inspector beside an always-visible world (B) or opaque content over a dimmed world (A)?
  3. Tracked robot in the live world (B) or a fixed, still avatar column (A)?
  4. Accordion-of-one with value strips (B) or section tabs (A)? Both mount one section at a time.
  5. Docked diagnostic feed for help (B) or popovers (A)?
  6. One-box selects (B) or single-line radio rows (A)? Select was retired for RadioButton in Phase 10.5.
  7. One lane dial per field (B) or the letter row (A)?
  8. Probes and Companies kept separate (B) or merged into a roster board (A)?
  9. Phone bottom sheet over the world (B) or a full page with a sticky avatar band (A)?

B1 — Deck (desktop): console strip, nothing else over the world

Five branch keys, the breadcrumb, telemetry, mute and a small volume strip in one 64 px strip. The power corner is unchanged. No welcome card; the copy moves to the off screen and the feed.

B2 — Robot detail (desktop): tracked robot on the stage, inspector on the right

The robot keeps working in the world with a halo and a name tag; the view pans to keep it in the open half. The inspector header carries name, status, battery and a one-box company select. Envelope is open; the other three show their values in one line. The diagnostic feed at the bottom describes the focused control (Decay Time).

B3 — Fleet Params → LFO Bank (desktop): one-box selects, lane dial, feed

Open accordion:

Lane and Shape are one box each and open a list. EQ shows one lane dial per band instead of a five-box picker and a depth slider. Closed groups still tell you their values.

B4 — Probes (desktop): compact rows, name tags on the stage

Rows are a small gem, name, job, status and a thin battery strip, with the company as a colour bar. A row click pans the stage to that robot. Companies remains its own branch with the same rows grouped and rename/delete at the top.

B5 — Phone: Deck, robot detail as a bottom sheet, Fleet Params sheet

The five keys are the bottom bar. The sheet sits at half height by default so the tracked robot stays visible above it; drag the handle for full height. Mute and power are the only things at the top.

Past the factories — the whole world

The 1920×1080 scene at half size: water column, seabed ridge, four midground factories, the stepped ground, then eleven scenery families in the foreground (pylon · tank · wall · containers · dome · scaffold · wreck · vent · crane · boulder · beacon) and two robots. Boulders, beacon and pylon heads are already gem shapes in the live app. The proposed rule: mass gets the ring, members stay lines. A mass is anything with a body (a tank, a wall, a dome's base, each container, a scaffold's solid block, a wreck's hull and deckhouse, each vent step, a crane's load); a member is a post, beam, mast, pylon tower or scaffold frame, which only gets the outline, and only if you turn it on. Terrain is its own switch: the ring runs along the ridge and ground profiles, so each 45° step and flat run takes the sun like a facet. The dome's curved cap stays smooth.

A mark is a reading to look at:
  • a value outside the written band (more than 2 % of its width past an edge), or values filling under 90 % of it;
  • a share off its expected value by more than 2 points or 4 standard errors (the Shimmer and Double shares expected after the Grit roll, every send's after the 1 % grid);
  • a Mix row the theme lights that is not lit in every world;
  • a grid cell whose theme sheet's Overlaps names neither the family nor any key taken;
  • a coverage key moved and not owned, or owned and not moved.

Gem controls: the crystal bar, and the rest of the primitives as facets

The control set in the gem material, to decide whether cabinetry can be let go. The bars are live: drag them with a mouse or a finger, or focus one and use the arrow keys. Companion to layout-rework (option A, gem ring on containers). Static otherwise; judge the shape, the readability of the lit / mid / dark split, and whether the axis is obvious.

G1 — SliderLinear: the crystal bar. Drag it.

Facets: Beam head:

A pointed rod, step-cut into facets. The beam enters at the left; passed facets are lit (two alternating close tones so the cuts read), the facet being crossed is mid, the rest are dark. The bright head is the handle, with a halo on hover and a stronger one while dragging. The exact value sits beside the bar as today.

G2 — SliderLog (tapering rod) and SliderCenteredZero (seam in the middle)

Log: the rod is thin at the low end and the facets grow geometrically, so a frequency slider looks like what it is. Centered zero: the beam starts at the seam and travels either way; at zero only the seam glows.

G3 — Vertical

The same rod stood on end, beam from the bottom. Drag up and down.

G4 — Toggle, RadioButton, Button: facets as units you choose

A toggle is a two-facet gem with one lit. A radio is a bar whose facets are the options. A button is one facet that lights while pressed. Click them.

G5 — Beside cabinetry: the same panel composed each way

Left: today's cabinetry inside a cabinet frame. Right: gem controls inside a gem container (the seeded chamfered polygon from the layout sketch). The question this frame asks is whether the right-hand panel is one material.

G6 — Phone

Bars at 44 px tall, edge to edge; vertical bars 44 px wide. Drag with a finger.

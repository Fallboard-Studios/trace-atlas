Robot halo + lit boundary lines — sketch

Gate sketch for a proposed Phase 41 on the gem robots. One robot from the seed, drawn three ways: world scale (2 px/unit, frame padded like the Phase 40 avatar), the 96 px detail avatar, and the 64 px card (static: halo and strips, no ripple, no arcs). The halo is one ellipse with a radial gradient whose stops are the robot's ADSR laid out along the radius; the ripple is a second ellipse with a narrow bright ring that travels outward during a spawn arc and inward during a despawn arc. Top and Mid boundary lines carry the Phase 40 light strip; their width follows that layer's gain-LFO depth. Any change to an attribute a line is tied to makes that strip flicker for two seconds on its own seeded pattern.

Gate questions: Does the halo read as light from behind the body rather than a disc? Does the ADSR ring shape read at world scale, and is the peak cap right against the lit Mids? Behind-the-backing or above it? Circle or stretched with the canvas? Does the ripple sell "the halo is sending the orbiter out / pulling it in", and is one cycle per second right? Do the Top/Mid strips at depth 0 (width 0.3) read as present? Does the flicker read as a reaction, not a fault?

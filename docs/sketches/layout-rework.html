Layout rework — five mockups, desktop and phone

Static mockups of the proposals from the 2026-10-08 screenshot review. Nothing is wired to the app; the cabinet pop, glow and wall fade are approximated. Judge structure and density, not the pixel finish. Each desktop frame is 1600 × 900 shown at 68 %; the phone frames are 375 × 812 at full size.

Open for Crawford:
  1. Rail + drawer, or a plain hamburger everywhere? (M1)
  2. World dimmed and blurred under an opaque content surface? (M2–M4)
  3. Section tabs, one section mounted at a time, replacing the accordion stack? (M2, M3) — reverses the 2026-09-24 stacked-scroll call.
  4. Nav tree cut to branch + entity? (M1 drawer)
  5. Single-line radio options, lore moved into the help popover? (M2, M3)
  6. Compact LfoLink: letter row + short depth strip? (M3)
  7. Roster board replacing both the Probes list and the Companies index? (M4)
  8. Phone: menu + breadcrumb in a bottom bar, mute + power at the top, volume flies out of mute? (M5)
  9. Off-screen label copy and the reserved picker slot? (M5)

M1 — Deck (desktop): rail nav, slim header, bright world, telemetry strip

The rail is 96 px and always visible. The tree slides out from it and stops at entity level. The telemetry strip carries what the nav status block showed. The hover label on a robot shows click-to-select exists.

M2 — Fleet Params → LFO Bank (desktop): opaque content, one intro, section tabs, help popover

One section mounted at a time; lanes are sub-tabs. Radio options are single-line. Voxel rows are 12 boxes in three columns. The lore name of each option lives in the popover, so the control row shrinks without losing it.

M3 — Robot detail (desktop): fixed avatar column, controls column, compact LfoLink

The avatar never scrolls. Meta sits in two columns under it with battery and company. The controls column has the same tab row as M2. Each linkable field carries a letter row and a short depth strip instead of a five-box two-line picker.

M4 — Roster board (desktop): Probes + Companies as one screen

Company headers are cabinet boxes in the company colour with rename, delete and bulk-edit behind them. Tiles are avatar, name, one status line and a thin battery strip. No per-card company radio; assignment is a move between groups.

M5 — Phone: off screen, Deck, robot detail, Fleet content

The header owns its 64 px band and content starts under it. Menu and breadcrumb live in a 56 px bottom bar. Voxel rows run edge to edge at 36 px boxes. The avatar band shrinks on scroll from 190 px to 58 px.

Close and Back — the row

Backlog 75, Gate 2. Every open screen gets a row with Back (the screen before, on the tabs it showed) and Close (the deck). The spec fixes what the two do and that the row is on every open screen and never on the deck; this page is for the look. The fixed panel (bottom right) switches every option; each frame's bar reads out what the row costs that screen. Sizes are the app's own (see the comment at the top of the file); the screens' contents are placeholders.

Open for Gate 2 (Crawford's three answers):
  1. The look: glyph, word, or both? And a lore line under each, or none (spec §7.2 Q5)?
  2. The place: at the top of the content plate (spec §7.1 B1, the default), or in the top band beside the header? The panel also has the split: band on the desk, plate on the phone.
  3. Does Back name where it goes ("Back to Probes"), or is it plain (spec §7.1 B2's default)?
What the readouts show, at a glance:
  • Plate: the row costs every screen one row (about 61 px), on both shells. It is below the power corner by construction: the plate starts at --top-bar-bottom, the lower of the header and the corner.
  • Top band, desk: free. With a screen open, the band between the rail and the header is empty (the meta box shows only on the deck), so the row sits there and the plate keeps its whole height.
  • Top band, phone: the band is full: the well over the ☰ / mute / ▾ panel, beside the corner. The two buttons go into that panel's line, which then needs 285 px as glyphs and has 225 px at 320 (280 at 375): it does not fit on either phone, and at 320 Close runs under the rocker. Hence the split option.

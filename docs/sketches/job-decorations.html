Job decorations — sketch

Six frames, one job each, looping. The shipped job timeline (bob, detach, moves, reattach) is replayed from the jobs sketch; the new part is the decoration drawn over the orbiters in each frame, in the robot's identity colour and the gem light colour, with no filters. Knobs in each frame's bar are the constants the real thing would carry. The panel bottom-right is page-wide.

Gate questions: Does each decoration say what the job is from across the world, or is it noise at world scale (try zoom 1)? Are they small enough — the brief was small? Do the wires (vent tethers, survey polygon, salvage load) read as wires or as stray lines? Does anything pop at a move boundary or at the reattach? Which rows change, which are cut?

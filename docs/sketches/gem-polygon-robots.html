Everything on this page comes from the seeded generator below, obeying the polygon rulebook (15° angles, 8–12 sides, ≤4 right angles, ≤2 concave corners on Top/Mids, ≤4 on orbiters, Backing convex, Top/Mids never symmetric, every polygon touches all four edges of its own box). Bevel ring = inset polygon + one trapezoid facet per edge, toned by that edge's outward normal against a single top-left light. Boundary lines live on the inner polygon, 0°/45°/90° only.

Gem Polygon Robots — sketch

Branch A gate for docs/ideas/gem-polygon-robots.md. Everything on this page comes from the seeded generator below, obeying the polygon rulebook (15° angles, 8–12 sides, ≤4 right angles, ≤2 concave corners on Top/Mids, ≤4 on orbiters, Backing convex, Top/Mids never symmetric, every polygon touches all four edges of its own box). Bevel ring = inset polygon + one trapezoid facet per edge, toned by that edge's outward normal against a single top-left light. Boundary lines live on the inner polygon, 0°/45°/90° only. Identity colours are the real 18 ACCENT_COLORS, no repeats across the 12.

Motion — orbiting polygons (Phase 40 gate)

Gate 1 for docs/specs/ORBITING_POLYGONS.md. One robot from the seed above, animated with plain JS here (the app uses GSAP). Orbiters drift near their corners, now and then ride a ring through the body — out along the line toward the canvas centre, behind the body, to the far side, and back in front (or the reverse) — and enter or leave by the first quarter of that ring, exactly on the centre line, so they vanish or appear at the canvas centre behind the body. (The x / y / z axis rings of the first draft ran along the canvas edges and never passed the body; replaced 2026-10-05.) Every dial comes from the composition settings on the left; the tuning constants on the right are the spec's defaults — move them, then write the verdict into this file's header comment. Left: world scale (2 px/unit) with the canvas outlined; middle: the 96 px detail avatar in its padded frame; right: the 64 px card, static.

World view: districts, new silhouettes, terrain, water — sketch

The app's 1920×1080 scene at half size, drawn from a seed the way the live placer draws a locale. The top stage is the one you tune; the strip below shows eight other seeds with the same settings, which is the real test: do different coordinates now look like different places? Everything is static, two-faced, 90/45 and single-colour, in the current palette. Hour drives the east/west faces exactly as getLighting does. The world is underwater: the surface is above the frame, the ridge is seabed, the tethers go up to it.

Gate questions: Do nine seeds read as nine places? Which of the families earn a spot, and in which depth row? Does the seabed ridge read as terrain rather than one more building? Do the light shafts sell "underwater" without fighting the depth gradients? Are derelict buildings and wrecks legible at night? Do the gem boulders rhyme with the robots or get mistaken for them?
